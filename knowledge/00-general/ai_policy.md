# AI-use disclosure — publisher constraints (Oct 2026; re-verify the live page before submission)

Common: AI is never an author; authors are accountable; AI may not create or alter research data or images except as a documented method; spelling/grammar tools are exempt; disclose by task, not just tool name. Never remove provenance marks or "AI-generated" labels to hide AI use.

| Publisher | Where | Must say |
|---|---|---|
| Elsevier | Heading "Declaration of Generative AI and AI-assisted technologies in the writing process" above References | "During the preparation of this work the author(s) used [TOOL] in order to [REASON]. After using this tool/service, the author(s) reviewed and edited the content as needed and take(s) full responsibility for the content of the publication." No AI images or graphical abstracts. |
| Springer Nature / Nature | Methods | Tool, version, how used; copy-editing of human text exempt; AI images generally not allowed. |
| Wiley | Methods or Acknowledgments | All AI use and purpose; no creating or manipulating data. |
| Taylor & Francis | Methods or Acknowledgments | Full tool name, version, how used, reason. |
| IEEE | Acknowledgments | AI system, the specific sections, level of use. |
| ACM | Acknowledgments | Which parts (text, tables, code, citations) were AI-generated. |
| Science / AAAS | Methods (research) + Acknowledgments (writing) + cover letter | Model name and version; AI images need editor permission. |
| PLOS | Methods subsection or Acknowledgments; figure legend for figure prep | Tools, how used, which parts, how outputs were validated. |
| MDPI | Acknowledgments + Materials and Methods | Tool and detailed how/which parts. |
| ICMJE 2026 | Cover letter + manuscript | Assert no plagiarism and no fabricated citations. |
| COPE | Methods or similar | Which tool, how used. |

Template (stage 08 fills it from provenance.json): "During the preparation of this work the authors used [Tool, version, vendor, access date] to [specific tasks by section]. No AI tool was used to generate, analyze, or alter research data, images, or figures. The authors reviewed, verified against primary sources, and edited all AI-assisted content, confirm that it contains no plagiarized material and no fabricated citations, and take full responsibility for the content of the publication." Place it per the table and add one sentence to the cover letter.
