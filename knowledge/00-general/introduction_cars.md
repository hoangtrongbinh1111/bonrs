# Introduction — CARS model (Swales) and gap logic

Move 1 Establish the territory: the specific problem (not "X is important to society"); the background a reader needs — only literature that serves the argument.
Move 2 Establish the niche: the gap. Known → unknown → needed. Gap types: population/context not covered, inconsistent findings, mechanism unclear, measurement/design limitation. Never "no study has ever…"; prefer "evidence remains limited on…", "few studies have examined…", "findings are inconsistent…", "it remains unclear whether…".
Move 3 Occupy the niche: purpose must mirror the gap exactly (gap about population ⇒ purpose about that population). State aim; objectives; research questions and/or hypotheses as the design requires (not all four mechanically). Significance: concrete contribution (theory/method/practice), no inflation.
Six-sentence pre-draft: What problem? What background? What has prior work shown? What is unclear? What will this study do? What does it add? Join these into paragraphs.
Introduction ≠ mini literature review: synthesize 3 agreeing studies into one claim; let disagreeing groups form the gap.
After Results/Discussion are written, re-check: gap still true? purpose matches analysis? no claim exceeds evidence? question asked at start is answered at end?
