# Reviewing like a PhD committee

Find every: unsupported assumption, leap in logic, bias risk, methodological flaw, over-generalisation, untraceable number, missing baseline, missing citation (only demand citations that actually exist), mismatch between abstract/intro claims and results, limitation the authors did not admit. Then ask the hardest questions a top-venue reviewer would ask. Score for THIS journal's bar, not an abstract ideal. Required-change list must be numbered, specific (section/line), and tagged MUST/SHOULD.
