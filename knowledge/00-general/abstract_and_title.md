# Abstract (10 checks) and title

1. Know why you publish and who reads it; 2. right venue (scope, audience, article type); 3. follow the venue's limits exactly (words, structured headings, keywords count, deadlines); 4. iterate several times, co-author sign-off; 5. concise: cut background, keep message; minimal abbreviations, define them; 6. follow reporting standards (CONSORT, PRISMA, STROBE, ARRIVE…) where applicable; 7. title 10–12 words naming object + approach; 8. structure: background → objective → methods (design, participants/data, measures) → key results (numbers) → conclusion (no overreach); 9. avoid: typos, over-length, trade names, salami slicing, undisclosed conflicts, abstract inconsistent with body; 10. get an outside read (the Writing reviewer seat).
