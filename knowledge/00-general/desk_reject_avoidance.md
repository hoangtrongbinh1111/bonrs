# Avoiding desk rejection (8 points)

1. Choose the journal before writing; read Aims & Scope and 5–10 recent papers; answer "why this journal and not another?".
2. Small details kill: word limits, structure, citation style, tables, ethics statements, COI, supplementary data — check Author Guidelines line by line.
3. Title + abstract + first intro paragraphs must let the editor see problem, gap, contribution in 2 minutes.
4. Method must carry the idea: justified sample size, validated measures, transparent processing, conclusions proportional to design (cross-sectional ≠ causal; many significant paths ≠ theoretical contribution).
5. Cover letter: problem, main contribution, why it fits this journal — concrete; originality and single-submission confirmation.
6. Re-read as a stranger: unclear logic, weak transitions, results not tied to hypotheses, contribution unconvincing; also references, tables, author info.
7. Never trade integrity: no plagiarism/self-plagiarism, no simultaneous submission, no data manipulation, no gift authorship; beware predatory venues.
8. Keep ~3 backup journals ranked; if desk-rejected, read the reason, fix positioning before resubmitting elsewhere.
