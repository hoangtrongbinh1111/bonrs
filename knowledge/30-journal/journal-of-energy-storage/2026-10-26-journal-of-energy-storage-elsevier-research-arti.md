Journal of Energy Storage (Elsevier) research article rules, guide accessed 2026-10-26:
- Scope gate: energy storage must be the central scientific contribution; optimisation techniques are not novelty and are discouraged in the title. Frame ML papers as battery-health insight, not a new network.
- Length: ideally <=6,000 words main text and 8-10 figures+tables total. Replication details and sensitivity analyses go in an Appendix (A, B; Eq. (A.1)), not Supplementary Material.
- Abstract <=250 words, standalone, no citations, abbreviations defined, give main findings with statistical significance where applicable.
- Keywords 1-7. Highlights mandatory: 3-5 bullets, each <=85 characters incl. spaces, separate file with 'highlights' in the filename. Graphical abstract optional (>=1328 px wide).
- LaTeX (elsarticle, elsarticle-num) or Word; editable source, not PDF. Numbered square-bracket citations in order of appearance; LTWA journal abbreviations; every reference must be real; datasets cited with [dataset] tag; preprints marked.
- Decimal section numbering; abstract unnumbered. 'Fig. N.' captions below, 'Table N' captions above; tables editable, no vertical rules or shading; figures as separate files (vector PDF/EPS, raster >=300 dpi photos, >=1000 dpi line art); do not combine unrelated graphs into one file.
- End matter before References: CRediT statement; Declaration of competing interest (plus Declarations tool .docx); Data availability (deposit and cite data/code or explain); Funding (or exact sentence 'This research did not receive any specific grant from funding agencies in the public, commercial, or not-for-profit sectors.'); GenAI section titled exactly 'Declaration of generative AI and AI-assisted technologies in the manuscript preparation process' with Elsevier template wording.
- Single-anonymised review, >=2 reviewers. No Part I/II papers. No resubmission after JES rejection unless invited. Hybrid OA, APC USD 4410.
- One English variant (British or American), not mixed.
- Accepted SOH papers: Introduction groups model-based vs data-driven (feature vs sequence) vs transfer; 3 bulleted contributions + an organisation paragraph; RMSE/MAE tables; usually no seeds or significance tests, so seeds, cell-wise splits and paired tests differentiate.
